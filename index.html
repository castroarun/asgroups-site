From a ₹6,000 salary to running five business operations.

Six years on, it has grown from one small store to outlets in Rathinamangalam, Mambakkam and a premium concept at Kandigai, which under his management has become the No. 1 Aladipattiyan branch in Chennai — with five more business operations alongside. AS Groups is how he now does the same for other businesses.
