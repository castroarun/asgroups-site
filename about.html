About AS Groups

Building smarter businesses. Together we grow.

AS Groups is a management and business consultancy in Chennai. We help people plan, set up, market and grow — cafés, restaurants, hospitals, retail and more.

The founder

From a ₹6,000 salary to running five business operations.

Harish VJ joined Aladipattiyan Karupatti Coffee in September 2020 on a monthly salary of ₹6,000. Six years on, it has grown from one small store to outlets in Rathinamangalam, Mambakkam and a premium concept at Kandigai, which under his management has become the No. 1 Aladipattiyan branch in Chennai — with five more business operations alongside.

AS Groups is how he now does the same for other businesses: a clear plan, complete execution, and support after opening day. We work with a network of trusted partners, so each project gets the right people for the job.
