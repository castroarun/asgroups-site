What we collect through this website and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you use the “Talk to us” form, you give us your name, phone number, the topic, your message and, if you choose, your email address.

How it reaches us

The form opens WhatsApp on your phone or computer with your message filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies to that chat.

How we use it

Keeping and deleting

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them for your enquiry or project. To ask us to delete them, message us on WhatsApp or email asgroupsofbusinessconsultant26@gmail.com.
